Aboutthe work

Dr. Jugul Kishor Gupta is a Professor and Head in the Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering, Bengaluru, with a research focus spanning RF and microwave engineering, antennas, MIMO systems, microwave circuits and wireless technologies.

His research work includes antenna design, dielectric resonator antennas, reconfigurable RF circuits, microwave filters, couplers and wireless communication systems, with collaborative work extending into AI/ML-based RF circuit optimization and 5G antenna research.
